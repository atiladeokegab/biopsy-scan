# Results

Every committed result, with its evidence label and source file. Labels: **MEASURED** (timed or counted on real runs), **ESTIMATE** (simulated, with an interval), **ANALYTIC** (from a formula), **SYNTHETIC** (simulated outcomes), **UNMEASURED** (not yet run). Real-cohort results are exploratory: the outcomes were already seen, so they make no clinical survival claim.

## Speed headline

**Headline speed, on `8` CPU cores (the `534×` fused arm ran on CPU; a GPU port is timed separately, see [Demo race replay](#demo-race-replay)):** `534×` vs off-the-shelf squidpy + lifelines, same host, median of `3` fresh containers (range `511–547×`; `22.35 s` per study over `48` studies vs `0.0426 s` per evaluated study over `3,000`, `8` workers each; timings **MEASURED**, outcomes **SYNTHETIC**; pipeline only, our reference recount and agreement checks timed separately, `547×` with them, the closest scope to the old `583×` combined-checks basis; `312×` on the same `48` studies; for `534×` and `312×` the external time is the whole off-the-shelf call per study (cells, squidpy, lifelines, Bonferroni call, each result returned to the parent) and the fused time is re-imaging and scoring only, the calibrated threshold and calls a separate step, excluded; different statistics, top pick the same in `16/16` on a matched config, [`docs/external-agreement.md`](docs/external-agreement.md); [`same-host-20261122T140908Z.json`](bench/results/same-host-20261122T140908Z.json)); `21×` vs an optimised lab-method rewrite (`0.881 s` vs `0.0419 s` per evaluated study, cross-run; `14.0×` per whole design point, `1,761.88 s` vs `125.73 s` compute wall; the fused time covers re-imaging and batched scoring; the cutoff and calls are outside the timing; **MEASURED**; [`modal-design-20261121T205536Z.json`](bench/results/modal-design-20261121T205536Z.json)); `5.5×` fair 8 v 8, compute per decided study with calibration charged, Numba arm, not the fused kernel (median of `3` repeats, range `4.6–5.6×`, **MEASURED**; [`sweep-repeats.json`](bench/results/sweep-repeats.json)). Rows below.

## Demo race replay

The race in the demo (`demo/app/`) replays two measurements taken in separate runs; it was not run on one machine. The left lane runs at `22.35 s` per study for the whole off-the-shelf squidpy + lifelines call, `8` CPU workers ([`same-host-20261122T140908Z.json`](bench/results/same-host-20261122T140908Z.json)). The right lane runs at `0.00207 s` per evaluation for re-imaging and scoring on one H100, the median of `3` timed runs of `3,000` evaluations each ([`gpu-fused-h100-20261122T141815Z.json`](bench/results/gpu-fused-h100-20261122T141815Z.json)). Their ratio, about `10,800×`, mixes hardware and timing windows: it sets the replay pace and isn't a like-for-like speedup. Timings **MEASURED**; the pace is computed in [`demo/app/data.json`](demo/app/data.json).
