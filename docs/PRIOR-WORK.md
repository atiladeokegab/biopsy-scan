# Prior Work Comparison: Spatial Study-Design Power and Survival Biomarker Screening

**Scope and labels:** Comparisons below scope Biopsy Scan's novelty strictly relative to the listed existing systems and pipelines. Every number carries its repository evidence label (**MEASURED**, **DERIVED**, **ESTIMATE**, **ANALYTIC**, **SYNTHETIC**, **TESTED**, or **UNMEASURED**). All real-cohort survival findings remain exploratory due to previously examined public outcomes.

## 1. Comparison Against Listed Existing Tools and Pipelines

| System / Method | What It Does (Cited) | What Biopsy Scan Does | What Only Biopsy Scan Answers (Among Listed Systems) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Baker et al. (2023) — `SpatialPower` / `PowerAnalysisForSpatialOmics`** ([Baker et al., *Nat Methods* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-01766-6); [`PMC9998272`](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9998272/); [GitHub](https://github.com/klarman-cell-observatory/PowerAnalysisForSpatialOmics)) | Synthesizes tunable *in silico* tissues on a random-circle-packing scaffold from pilot cell-type abundances and pairwise neighborhood distributions ($H$). Studies **acquisition-dependent spatial power** (FOV size, number of FOVs/sections, spatial resolution) for **cell-type discovery** (beta-binomial / negative-binomial abundance detection) and single-pair neighborhood enrichment tests. Models cell-type mis-calling analytically by down-weighting target abundance via an abundance FDR factor. Does **not** fit censored Cox survival models (`Cox`: 0 hits in `PMC9998272`) or run FWER-controlled multi-pattern survival screening (`FWER` / `Bonferroni`: 0 hits in `PMC9998272`). | Resamples **real patient CODEX tissue** (`Charville`: `159` patients, `286` regions, `619,645` cells, **MEASURED**) without discarding spatial histology; applies explicit random FOV cropping, region sub-sampling ($k$), and cell-by-cell confusion-matrix mis-calling ($\varepsilon$, [`core/reimage_ref.py`](../core/reimage_ref.py)). Uses three distinct analysis tracks: (a) the `630`-feature (`v1`) **Atlas simulation** planting Weibull PH survival + stratum reverse-KM censoring ([`atlas/simulation.py`](../atlas/simulation.py)) and rejecting above a shared unconditional design `maxT` cutoff ([`stats/reference.py`](../stats/reference.py)); (b) the `3,780`-feature (`v1+v2`) real-cohort exploratory audit on Charville ([`results/charville-v2.json`](../results/charville-v2.json)); and (c) the **R-15 clinical-adjusted parametric bootstrap** screen ([`stats/screen.py`](../stats/screen.py)). | How many **patients** and **FOV / tissue regions per patient** ($n, k, \text{FOV}, \varepsilon$) a **time-to-event survival study** needs to clear **FWER-calibrated (`alpha = 0.05`) `630`-pattern biomarker screening** when cell-type classification errors (`eps = 0.1`) and sub-core cropping propagate through spatial features (`n_80 ~ 524` on Charville, **ESTIMATE**; [`bench/results/power-at-scale.json`](../bench/results/power-at-scale.json), whereas whole-tissue analytic Schoenfeld + Bonferroni predicts `80.1%` power at the evaluated `n = 200` point vs. `27.0%` [95% CI `22.1%, 32.4%`] **ESTIMATE** simulated; [`bench/results/vs-schoenfeld-300.json`](../bench/results/vs-schoenfeld-300.json)). Downstream 1-/2-stain IHC panel translation (`translate/`) is specified in code, with execution currently pending (**UNMEASURED**, [`results/upmc-translate.json`](../results/upmc-translate.json)). |
| **`permGPU`** ([Shterev et al., *BMC Bioinformatics* 2010](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-11-329); [`PMC2910023`](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2910023/); [CRAN archive](https://cran.r-project.org/src/contrib/Archive/permGPU/)) | CUDA/C++/R engine computing 6 test statistics—including censored **Cox score** and **Cox rank score** statistics—across a **single fixed $n \times K$ microarray matrix** under global row permutation, returning Westfall–Young (`1993`) single-step `maxT` FWER-adjusted $p$-values. Does **not** simulate spatial tissue re-imaging, FOV cropping, or label mis-calling (`spatial`: 0 hits in `PMC2910023`), does not run stratified or nuisance-adjusted clinical-null bootstrap resampling, and leaves multi-study power simulation unimplemented (discussed in `PMC2910023` only as a conceptual outer loop repeating permutation analysis $N$ times). | Executes **cell-level spatial re-imaging** (Numba CPU / CUDA [`reimage/`](../reimage/) + CSR prefix counting) coupled with Cox score evaluation across **thousands of resampled Atlas studies** (`calibration=10,000`, `null=10,000`, `alternative=1,000` per design point, `630` features, **SYNTHETIC** outcomes). Separately, for cohort screening (R-15 [`stats/screen.py`](../stats/screen.py)), handles per-feature missingness (`NaN`) masks without imputation, Schur-complement nuisance adjustment (`V_j = I_jj - I_jC I_CC^-1 I_Cj`), and `clinical-null parametric bootstrap` calibration (`60/1,000 = 0.060` CP 95% `[0.0461, 0.0766]` vs `0.997` unadjusted on confounded A-15.6 negative control, **SYNTHETIC**, [`stats/results/hard-negative-f458ad1.json`](../stats/results/hard-negative-f458ad1.json)). | Study-level **power and empirical FWER surfaces over physical pathology acquisition choices** (`FOV`, `regions_per_patient`, `eps`) prior to imaging, plus **FWER-calibrated clinical biomarker screens when candidates correlate with clinical covariates** (e.g., HPV status) and contain incomplete spatial masks. On multi-GPU scaling, shards `1,000` Charville re-imaging/feature-generation studies across `8` vs `1` A100-SXM4-40GB GPUs at **`6.3x` feature-generation compute-span reduction** (median of 2 fresh repeats, range `6.18-6.42x`, excluding survival scoring, **MEASURED**, [`bench/results/sweep-repeats.json`](../bench/results/sweep-repeats.json)). |
| **Standard survival pipelines (`statsmodels` `PHReg`, R `survival` `coxph`, `lifelines` `CoxPHFitter`)** ([Seabold & Perktold 2010](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.duration.hazard_regression.PHReg.html); [Therneau `survival`](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=survival); [Davidson-Pilon, *JOSS* 2019](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.01317)) + off-the-shelf `squidpy` / analytic Schoenfeld ([Schoenfeld 1983](https://doi.org/10.2307/2531021); [Hsieh & Lavori 2000](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0197-2456(00)00104-5)) | Fit one-study-at-a-time **stratified Cox proportional hazards MLEs** (`PHReg` serves as Biopsy Scan's exact Breslow tie-handling **score numerator `U` unit-test oracle** to `atol=2e-14` in [`stats/test_reference.py`](../stats/test_reference.py), **TESTED**), paired externally with `statsmodels.stats.multitest.multipletests` or R `p.adjust`. Bonferroni (`alpha / m`) **controls FWER under arbitrary dependence** via union bound, though losing power on correlated features; analytic Cox power (`lifelines.statistics.power_under_cph` / Schoenfeld–Hsieh–Lavori + Bonferroni) uses closed-form whole-tissue assumptions without imaging error. On `3,780` Charville patterns, full per-candidate `PHReg` fitting takes `63.84 s` on 8 workers (`298.30 s` on 1 worker, **MEASURED**, [`bench/results/impl-a-charville-amd-epyc-7b13.json`](../bench/results/impl-a-charville-amd-epyc-7b13.json)); an off-the-shelf `squidpy + lifelines + Bonferroni` pipeline takes `24.44 s/study` **MEASURED** pool wall including instrumented reference recount/agreement checks (`21.42 s/study` **DERIVED** extrapolation: summed worker time divided by `8` workers assuming ideal utilization) on 8 cores ([`bench/results/external-baseline-20261122T043823Z.json`](../bench/results/external-baseline-20261122T043823Z.json)). | Replaces $m$ iterative MLE loops with a single risk-set pass and matrix product (`U = X'm`): with zero resamples (`n_perm = 0`), scoring `3,780` features takes median `0.803 s` vs `32.36 s` paired median full per-feature `PHReg` (`40.3x` **workflow comparison** of different statistical outputs at zero resamples, **MEASURED**, [`bench/results/screen-vs-r14.json`](../bench/results/screen-vs-r14.json)). Replaces Bonferroni with correlated Westfall–Young `maxT` resampling ([Westfall & Young 1993](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Resampling+Based+Multiple+Testing%3A+Examples+and+Methods+for+p+Value+Adjustment-p-9780471557616)). With fused Numba re-imaging (`0.0419 s/study`, **MEASURED**), contrasts against off-the-shelf `squidpy + lifelines` at **`534x` same host** (median of the paired per-repeat ratios over 3 fresh containers, range `511-547x`, `22.35 s/study` over 48 external studies, the whole off-the-shelf call, vs `0.0426 s` per evaluated study over 3,000 fused evaluations, re-imaging and scoring with the threshold and calls excluded, different procedures, **MEASURED**, [`bench/results/same-host-20261122T140908Z.json`](../bench/results/same-host-20261122T140908Z.json)), and against the hand-written lab arm (`0.881 s/study`) at **`21.0x` cross-run** ([`bench/results/modal-design-20261121T205536Z.json`](../bench/results/modal-design-20261121T205536Z.json)) and **`25.2x` ratio of arm medians** across 3 fresh-container repeats per arm (descriptive range `25.0-29.9x` from extreme pairs, comparing different procedures, **MEASURED**, [`bench/results/repeats-20261121T2209Z.json`](../bench/results/repeats-20261121T2209Z.json)). | Quantifies **how far textbook Schoenfeld + Bonferroni formulas overstate real spatial biomarker study power** (`n = 200`, `HR = 2.0/SD`, `M = 630`, `alpha = 0.05`: Schoenfeld predicts `80.1%` **ANALYTIC** vs `27.0%` [95% CI `22.1%, 32.4%`] **ESTIMATE** in cell-by-cell Charville simulation, overstating power at `6` of `7` evaluated patient counts; [`bench/results/vs-schoenfeld-300.json`](../bench/results/vs-schoenfeld-300.json)). Also reveals procedure-level differences invisible to single-fit tools, such as shared unconditional design calibration vs per-study conditional permutation power (`15.3%` vs `20.3%`, **ESTIMATE**, [`bench/results/modal-design-20261121T194458Z.json`](../bench/results/modal-design-20261121T194458Z.json)). |
